# Mind.exe (Motion Design)

## Project Idea
The project explores the impact of modern technology and artificial intelligence on the human mind, and the negative changes they may cause in the way we think, perceive, and behave. It highlights the phenomenon of "brain rot" and its effects on individuals and society amid rapid technological advancement.
